StreetWatch — a live console for the public planet

Watch the world through 7,000+ public live feeds: flight radar, ship positions, weather satellites, public webcams, wildlife cameras and the ISS — and a capability nobody else runs: a 24/7 military & drone watch sweeping 1,039 airspaces across 169 countries, with a 90-day public archive of every military and UAV contact, replayable on a map.

What makes it different

Honesty. Public data has hard edges — aircraft flying with transponders off are invisible, submarines cannot be tracked by anyone because radio does not travel through seawater, and marine AIS coverage varies by region. Where StreetWatch cannot see, it says so, on every display and in every API response. Public feeds only; never private cameras.

What you can do

  • Browse the whole catalog on a clustered world map with live drone, activity-heat, sea-drone and submarine-support layers
  • Open any airport's radar and filter to military or UAV traffic
  • Replay archived drone tracks over real geography with a timeline
  • Ask in plain English ("drone activity near the Black Sea last week") and get any archived track explained by AI-assisted analysis — every figure computed first, every AI summary labelled
  • See live ships in the Baltic and along the Norwegian coast from state-run open feeds
  • Find public webcams within 50 km of anything
