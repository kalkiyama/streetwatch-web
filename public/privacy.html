StreetWatch — Privacy Policy

Effective: July 18, 2026 · Applies to the StreetWatch web app and Android app.

The short version

StreetWatch has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no database of users. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. There is nothing to breach because we hold nothing.

Location ("Near me")

If you tap Near me, your browser or device asks your permission to read your approximate location. That location is used only on your device, in the moment, to sort feeds by distance. It is never transmitted to our servers, never stored, and never shared. Declining permission simply disables the feature.

Favorites

Feeds you star are saved in your own browser's local storage on your device. They never leave it.

What our servers see

When the app shows live aircraft or ships, it asks our proxy server for public transponder data near a map coordinate. Like any web server, ours momentarily processes your IP address to answer the request and enforce fair-use rate limits (counts are kept in memory for about one minute, then discarded). We keep no logs tying IP addresses to activity, and no profiles of any kind.

Data sources

All content shown is published for public viewing by its providers: aircraft positions (airplanes.live), ship positions (aisstream.io, Fintraffic), satellite imagery (NASA), ISS position (wheretheiss.at), map tiles (© OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO), and links to public traffic and webcam sites operated by their respective authorities. Opening those external sites is subject to their own privacy policies. StreetWatch shows public feeds only and never accesses private cameras.

Children

StreetWatch does not target children and, collecting no data, gathers none from anyone.
